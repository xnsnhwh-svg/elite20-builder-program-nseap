# Bilingual Translation: The Future of Education - Symbiotic Intelligence and the AI+X Paradigm

## Abstract / 摘要

**English:**
The accelerating integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into all domains of work and society has exposed a fundamental misalignment between traditional educational models and the competencies needed in an AI-driven world. This position paper proposes the AI+X paradigm as a comprehensive framework to re-architect education for symbiotic human-AI collaboration. We outline a three-dimensional learning objective system encompassing disciplinary world-modeling, AI collaboration fluency, and metacognitive self-learning, enabled by a dual-loop (conceptual and procedural) pedagogy mediated by intelligent agents. We argue that this approach compresses time-to-productivity, mitigates demographic challenges in advanced economies by shortening the education-to-work pipeline, and fosters a new generation of “Super Individuals” capable of orchestrating human-AI intelligence for creativity and problem-solving. The paper presents the theoretical foundations of Symbiotic Intelligence, details the AI+X methodology and infrastructure (including a cognitive architecture and emerging international standards), and highlights a large-scale case study of implementation at SIAS University. We conclude with policy and institutional recommendations, positioning AI+X education as a strategic catalyst for socio-economic resilience and a step toward a symbiotic human-AI civilization.

**中文 (简体):**
人工智能（AI）加速融入工作和社会的各个领域，暴露了传统教育模式与AI驱动世界所需能力之间的根本错位。本立场文件提出了 **AI+X 范式**，作为重新构建教育以实现人机协同共生的综合框架。我们概述了一个三维学习目标体系，包括学科 **世界建模**、AI协作熟练度和元认知 **自我建模**，并由智能体调节的（概念和程序）**双回路教学法** 提供支持。我们认为，这种方法压缩了从学习到产出的时间，通过缩短教育到工作的路径缓解了发达经济体的人口挑战，并培养了新一代能够统筹人机智能以进行创意和问题解决的“**双脑人才**”。本文介绍了 **共生智能** 的理论基础，详细阐述了 **AI+X 范式** 的方法论和基础设施（包括认知架构和新兴的国际标准），并重点介绍了在西亚斯学院（SIAS University）进行的大规模实施案例研究。最后，我们提出了政策和制度建议，将 AI+X 教育定位为社会经济韧性的战略催化剂，以及迈向人机共生文明的一步。

## Section 1: Introduction: The Imperative for a New Educational Paradigm / 第一节：引言：新教育范式的紧迫性

**English:**
The prevailing industrial-age model of education is not merely outdated; it is structurally incapable of preparing learners for the complexities of the emerging era of Symbiotic Intelligence (SI). An alarming alignment crisis has unfolded—a widening chasm between what traditional schooling produces and the skills demanded by a world increasingly defined by human-AI cognitive partnerships. This alignment gap is more than a pedagogical concern; it poses a socio-economic and demographic threat to the stability of advanced economies. Educational outcomes focused on static knowledge and test scores are misaligned with a labor market that values adaptability, creative problem-solving, and the ability to leverage AI as a collaborator. To bridge this gap, we must fundamentally reorient education from its 20th-century focus on knowledge transmission to a 21st-century model centered on the orchestration of intelligence.

**中文 (简体):**
盛行的工业时代教育模式不仅已经过时，而且在结构上无法让学习者应对新兴的 **共生智能**（Symbiotic Intelligence, SI）时代的复杂性。一场令人担忧的对齐危机已经展开——传统学校教育的产出与一个日益由人机认知伙伴关系定义的场景所需技能之间，存在着日益扩大的鸿沟。这种对齐差距不仅是教学上的隐忧，它还对发达经济体的稳定构成了社会经济和人口学上的威胁。专注于静态知识和考试分数的教育成果，与重视适应性、创造性问题解决以及利用 AI 作为合作伙伴能力的劳动力市场之间发生了错位。为了弥合这一差距，我们必须从根本上重新调整教育方向，从 20 世纪侧重于知识传递的模式，转变为以 21 世纪智能统筹为中心的模式。

### 1.1 Exponential Change and the Human–AI Partnership / 1.1 指数级变革与人机伙伴关系

**English:**
Recent advances in AI—especially generative AI—have transformed it from a niche tool into a pervasive cognitive partner in nearly every field. AI systems can now produce human-like text, code, designs, and decisions, reshaping the nature of intellectual labor and professional work. Unlike previous technologies that mainly automated physical or routine tasks, modern AI encroaches on complex cognitive tasks in white-collar domains, disrupting job roles that were once considered the exclusive province of human experts. Traditional curricula, however, still prioritize content memorization and standardized testing, metrics that are increasingly irrelevant in an AI-augmented landscape.

**中文 (简体):**

Companies adopting such “obelisk organizations” can achieve unprecedented efficiency and innovation. Prolonged educational pathways mean young adults remain economically dependent well into their third decade of life, postponing key life milestones—entering stable employment, marriage, and parenthood—resulting in an aging population with fewer children.

**中文 (简体):**

## Section 2: The AI+X Theoretical Framework: Defining Symbiotic Intelligence / 第二节：AI+X 理论框架：定义共生智能

**English:**
The AI+X paradigm is built upon the foundation of **Symbiotic Intelligence (SI)**. SI is not about AI replacing humans, but about a synergy where both human and AI intelligences are amplified through collaboration. This framework is characterized by **Dynamic Functorism**, which views knowledge not as static facts but as dynamic relationships and transformations that can be modeled and shared between humans and AI.

**中文 (简体):**
**AI+X 范式** 建立在 **共生智能 (SI)** 的基础之上。共生智能的核心并非 AI 取代人类，而是通过协作使人类和 AI 的智能都得到增强。该框架的特点是 **动态函子主义 (Dynamic Functorism)**，它将知识视为动态关系和转换，而不是静态事实，并可以在人类与 AI 之间进行建模和共享。

### 2.1 Symbiotic Intelligence: Human–AI Cognitive Synergy / 2.1 共生智能：人机认知协同

**English:**
Symbiotic Intelligence represents a state where the cognitive capabilities of a human-AI system exceed the sum of its individual parts. This synergy is achieved through continuous interaction, where the human provides intuition, creativity, and ethical judgment, while the AI provides data processing, pattern recognition, and rapid execution. The result is a more powerful and adaptive form of intelligence capable of tackling complex, real-world challenges.

**中文 (简体):**
**共生智能** 代表了人机系统中认知能力超过其各个部分总和的状态。这种协同作用是通过持续的互动实现的：人类提供直觉、创造力和伦理判断，而 AI 提供数据处理、模式识别和快速执行。其结果是一种更强大、更具适应性的智能形式，能够应对复杂的现实挑战。

## Section 3: What to Learn: The Three Dimensions of Learning Objectives / 第三节：学什么：学习目标的三大维度

**English:**
The AI+X framework defines three core dimensions of learning that are essential for success in a symbiotic world. These dimensions move beyond traditional subject-based learning and focus on the skills needed for effective human-AI collaboration and lifelong learning.

**中文 (简体):**
**AI+X 范式** 定义了在共生世界中取得成功必不可少的三个核心学习维度。这些维度超越了传统的基于学科的学习，重点关注有效的人机协作和终身学习所需的技能。

### 3.1 L1: Disciplinary Knowledge as World Modeling / 3.1 L1：作为世界建模的学科知识

**English:**
In the AI+X paradigm, disciplinary knowledge is re-envisioned as **World Modeling**. Instead of memorizing facts, students learn to build conceptual models of their field. This involves understanding the core principles, relationships, and dynamics of a discipline, allowing them to communicate effectively with AI and provide it with the necessary context for problem-solving.

**中文 (简体):**
在 **AI+X 范式** 中，学科知识被重新构想为 **世界建模**。学生不再仅仅记忆事实，而是学习构建其领域的概念模型。这包括理解学科的核心原则、关系和动态，使他们能够有效地与 AI 交流，并为 AI 提供解决问题所需的环境。

### 3.2 L2: AI Collaboration Skills as Human–AI Co-Modeling / 3.2 L2：作为人机协同建模的 AI 协作技能

**English:**
The second dimension focus on **Human-AI Co-modeling**. This involves developing the skills to work effectively with AI as a partner. Students learn how to articulate problems, provide feedback, and critically evaluate AI outputs. This collaborative modeling process allows humans and AI to iteratively refine their understanding and reach more robust solutions.

**中文 (简体):**
第二个维度的重点是 **人机协同建模**。这包括培养与 AI 作为伙伴有效协作的技能。学生学习如何清晰地表达问题、提供反馈并批判性地评估 AI 的输出。这种协作建模过程允许人类和 AI 反复完善他们的理解，并达成更可靠的解决方案。

### 3.3 L3: Metacognitive Intelligence as Self-Modeling / 3.3 L3：作为自我建模的元认知智能

**English:**
The third dimension, **Self-modeling**, focuses on metacognitive intelligence. This involves the ability to reflect on one's own learning process, identify strengths and weaknesses, and adapt learning strategies accordingly. In a rapidly changing world, the ability to learn how to learn and continuously update one's skills is the most critical competency of all.

**中文 (简体):**
第三个维度是 **自我建模**，侧重于元认知智能。这包括反思自身学习过程的能力，识别优势和劣势，并据此调整学习策略。在一个快速变化的世界中，学习如何学习并不断更新自身技能的能力，是所有能力中最关键的一项。

## Section 4: How to Learn: The Agentic Dual-Loop Methodology / 第四节：如何学：智能体双回路方法论

**English:**
The AI+X framework is implemented through an **Agentic Dual-Loop Methodology**. This methodology integrates the three learning dimensions (L1, L2, L3) into a cohesive, iterative learning experience. Two core design principles anchor this: (1) the use of specialized AI agents to scaffold different stages of learning, and (2) the separation and integration of conceptual learning vs. procedural execution (the **Dual-loop Pedagogy**).

**中文 (简体):**
**AI+X 范式** 通过 **智能体双回路方法论** 实施。该方法论将三个学习维度（L1、L2、L3）整合为一个连贯的、迭代的学习体验。两个核心设计原则支撑了这一点：(1) 使用专门的 AI 智能体来支撑学习的不同阶段；(2) 概念学习与程序执行（即 **双回路教学法**）的分离与整合。

### 4.1 Three-Stage Agentic Learning Cycle / 4.1 三阶段智能体学习周期

**English:**
In AI+X, learning is a continuous cycle of preparation, performance, and reflection, with each stage facilitated by AI agents playing distinct roles:
1. **Stage 1: Socratic Playground of Learning (Pre-task, Conceptual Focus).** An AI **Socratic Learning Companion** helps transform passive study into interactive, inquiry-driven exploration. It ensures that students build a robust mental model (world model) of the knowledge before engaging in a complex task.
2. **Stage 2: Interactive Learning Task (Task execution).** The core "learning by doing" stage, where students tackle authentic, open-ended tasks as **Interactive Learning Tasks (ILT)**. An AI **Co-Creative Task Agent** acts as a brainstorming and execution partner throughout the task.
3. **Stage 3: Reflective Review (Post-task, Metacognitive Focus).** After task completion, an AI **Reflective Companion** guides the student through an **After-Action Review (AAR)**, helping them analyze their performance, reflect on their learning process, and identify areas for improvement (addressing L3).

**中文 (简体):**
在 AI+X 中，学习是一个由准备、执行和反思组成的持续循环，每个阶段都由扮演不同角色的 AI 智能体协助：
1. **第一阶段：苏格拉底式学习乐园 (任务前，侧重概念)。** AI **苏格拉底式学习伙伴** 帮助将被动学习转变为互动式、探究驱动的探索。它确保学生在参与复杂任务之前，已建立起对知识的稳健心理模型（世界模型）。
2. **第二阶段：互动学习任务 (任务执行)。** 核心的“在做中学”阶段，学生以 **互动学习任务 (ILT)** 的形式处理真实的、开放式的任务。AI **共创任务智能体** 在整个任务过程中充当头脑风暴和执行伙伴。
3. **第三阶段：反思性评价 (任务后，侧重元认知)。** 任务完成后，AI **反思伙伴** 引导学生进行 **课后复盘 (AAR)**，帮助他们分析自己的表现，反思学习过程并找出改进之处（针对 L3）。

### 4.2 Conceptual vs. Procedural Dual-Loop Design / 4.2 概念回路与程序回路设计

**English:**
The Dual-Loop structure separates and then integrates the "why" (conceptual) and the "how" (procedural). The conceptual loop focuses on building deep understanding and world modeling (L1), while the procedural loop focus on execution, AI collaboration, and co-modeling (L2). This allows learners to gain mastery of core principles while simultaneously developing the skills to leverage AI for complex task execution.

**中文 (简体):**
双回路结构将“为什么”（概念性）和“怎么做”（程序性）分离并随后整合。概念回路专注于建立深度理解和世界建模 (L1)，而程序回路专注于执行、AI 协作和协同建模 (L2)。这使得学习者在掌握核心原则的同时，也培养了利用 AI 执行复杂任务的技能。

### 4.3 From Exams to Evidence: Transforming Assessment / 4.3 从考试到证据：评估转型

**English:**
Traditional exams are replaced by **Portfolio Assessment**. In AI+X, assessment is based on the evidence of learning and problem-solving captured in a student's portfolio. This includes draft versions, AI interaction logs, reflective summaries, and final artifacts. This approach provides a much more holistic and accurate picture of a student's true capabilities than a single test score.

**中文 (简体):**
传统的考试被 **作品集评估** 所取代。在 AI+X 中，评估基于学生作品集中捕捉到的学习和解决问题的证据。这包括草案版本、AI 互动日志、反思总结和最终产出。与单一的考试分数相比，这种方法能更全面、更准确地反映学生的真实能力。

## Section 6.3: Practical Implementation: The SIAS University Case Study / 第 6.3 节：实践应用：西亚斯学院案例研究

**English:**
The AI+X paradigm has been under continuous institutional implementation at SIAS University (Henan, China) since early 2025. Led by Dr. Shawn Chen and the NEOLAF team, the transformation proceeded in three sequenced phases:
1. **Phase 1: Institutional Experiment.** Rewriting policies and assessment rules to admit AI as a legitimate co-producer.
2. **Phase 2: Faculty Training at Scale.** Moving faculty from lecture-centric delivery to mentor-and-orchestrator roles through workshops and co-design of Interactive Learning Tasks.
3. **Phase 3: The Elite 20 Program.** A flagship cohort running the full AI+X curriculum, including L1 world-modeling, L2 human-AI co-modeling, and L3 self-modeling.

**中文 (简体):**
自 2025 年初以来，**AI+X 范式** 已在西亚斯学院（中国河南）进行了持续的制度性实施。在陈肖纯博士和 **NEOLAF (永续开放学习智能体框架)** 团队的领导下，这一转型按三个阶段有序进行：
1. **第一阶段：机构实验。** 重写政策和评估规则，承认 AI 为合法的共同生产者。
2. **第二阶段：大规模教师培训。** 通过研讨会和共同设计 **互动学习任务 (ILT)**，将教学队伍从以讲座为中心的授课模式转变为导师和统筹者的角色。
3. **第三阶段：精英 20 项目 (Elite 20 Program)。** 一个运行完整 AI+X 课程的旗舰班级，包括 L1 **世界建模**、L2 **人机协同建模** 和 L3 **自我建模**。

**English:**
The implementation utilizes the **NEOLAF** agent framework and the **KSTAR (Knowledge, Situation, Task, Action, Result)** cognitive architecture to support personalized, adaptive learning and provide human-auditable logs of the learning process. Students in the Elite 20 program have demonstrated the ability to produce professional-grade artifacts at a level far exceeding traditional expectations.

**中文 (简体):**
实施过程中利用了 **NEOLAF** 智能体框架和 **KSTAR (知识-情境-任务-行动-结果)** 认知架构，以支持个性化的自适应学习，并提供学习过程中可供人类审计的日志。精英 20 项目的学生已展示出能够产出专业级作品的能力，其水平远超传统预期。
